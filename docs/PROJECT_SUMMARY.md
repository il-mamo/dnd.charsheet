## Purpose and confirmed scope
Private-use Windows desktop character manager, strictly PHB 2024. Full classes/subclasses, species, backgrounds, feats, spells and equipment through levels 1–20, including multiclassing. Player-only perspective; Adventures contain one or more characters, each assigned to at most one Adventure with explicit transfer; Adventure deletion retains unassigned characters, unavailable for play until reassigned. Adventure selection is required at every launch; management is in scope (R11, not implemented). Multiple characters, portraits, guided/existing entry, sheet/finder, battle assistance, inventory and resources. All additional P01–P07 features accepted: autosave/recovery, rests/conditions, advancement, casting resources, backup/restore, accessibility, currency/consumables and session history. DM adjudicates hit/success. No Jira.

## Confirmed stack and defaults
C#/.NET + WPF/MVVM + SQLite; offline, local single-user operation. Windows 11 x64 default target. SDK 10.0.402 pinned; CommunityToolkit.Mvvm 8.4.2, Microsoft.Data.Sqlite 10.0.13 and MSTest 4.0.2 centrally pinned. Owner selected self-contained win-x64 ZIP with manual updates. PDF/private catalog excluded from GitHub. Other defaults are in specification section 2; they may be revised by owner instruction.
