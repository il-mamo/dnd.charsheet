## Keyboard and accessibility

Initial focus remains Search after successful nonempty load and moves to Create only for a true empty result. Arrow keys move card selection; Space selects. Selecting, hovering or double-clicking a card does not open it. Open requires its explicit focused button/command. Escape closes create/management transient presentation and restores focus to its invoker. The existing shared-view query suspension/restoration and live-region announcements remain.

All functional text sits on opaque panels. The title remains text rather than baked into the background. Generated art, readability wash, rule, panel corners, image placeholders and button icons are nonessential, non-hit-testable and excluded from the accessibility control view. Brass decoration does not replace the blue focus ring. High Contrast hides the bitmap and normal-mode gradients, uses system Window/WindowText/Highlight/HighlightText/focus resources, and retains visible one-color vector boundaries. No state depends on art or color alone.
